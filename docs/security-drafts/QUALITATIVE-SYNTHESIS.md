# Inactive qualified interpretation candidate

This local change is separate from the completed QBO/Square Sandbox admission
bridge. It reuses currently authorized evidence and the same manifests, saves,
citations and accounting. It adds no SQL, reader, grant, ingestion or model call.
`qualitativeSynthesisEnabled()` is false; production and Sandbox gates stay closed.

## Changed restriction

Factual claims and deterministic financial/numeric relationships keep their
existing validation. They do not gain a general multi-source prose exception.
An additional structured `interpretations` array permits a novel qualified
hypothesis without matching predefined relationship text. Its exact premises,
missing information and next check are supplied separately. Examples tested are
reviewing payment status alongside accrual basis, and whether reported staffing
coverage might be relevant to reported delivery delays. Neither asserts a cause,
record match or measured business outcome.

The application checks complete issued facts, exact content/source bindings and
distinct original dependencies. Different facts from one underlying row cannot
serve as distinct dependencies. Source modes/installations cannot mix. Each
hypothesis must use business vocabulary from every premise, including a distinct
anchor from each, and explicit may/might/could qualification. Attribution occurs
only in application-owned premise labels. Every authored field is checked;
numbers, causal assertions, matching/attribution assertions, new business terms,
extra sentences and unsupported character sequences are rejected. The bounded
authored-language policy is English with common punctuation. Original quoted
evidence is preserved, including its original numbers and qualifiers.

Accepted text is application-rendered as an **unverified hypothesis**, followed by
the attributed premises, missing information, next check and a scope disclaimer.
No interpretation counts toward measured-claim minimums. Brief sections retain
their ordinary factual summary and append the interpretation with all support
references and structured metadata. Storage reparses that metadata and rejects
altered bindings or text. Executive summaries do not promote hypotheses to facts.
VSI saves the complete labeled block and original citation hashes; existing history
requires fresh authorization, and assistant answers are not independent evidence.
Saved copies retain the labeled prose and existing inherited-source restrictions.

The brief prompt, validator and generation policy advance to v7. Historical v6
artifacts remain readable; no historical artifact is rewritten. An omitted
interpretations array is treated as empty for existing callers/artifacts, while
new provider requests specify the array explicitly.

## Unchanged controls

- Live actor/session, owner audience, workspace, installation and environment
  boundaries; original-actor private VSI copies and current source eligibility.
- Exact manifest dependencies, pre-provider validation, protected settlement,
  source revocation, exact saved citation reopening and once-only accounting.
- Calculation recipes, currency/unit/period/coverage requirements and refusal to
  add overlapping payment/accounting evidence. No labor ratio or item matching
  authority is introduced. Reported files/notes remain reported context.
- Square's existing non-economic and disabled model-dispatch contract; this patch
  does not approve the separate descriptive Sandbox model-use proposal.

## Local evidence and acceptance limit

The unit fixture tests novel file/note hypotheses and negative source, quote,
dependency, vocabulary, numeric, attribution, causal, hidden-join, Unicode and
environment cases. The actual mocked VSI/brief fixture tests a novel two-plugin
hypothesis through generation validation, protected SQL save, stored artifact
parsing and citation reopening. It also verifies that interpretations cannot
increase measured-claim counts, closed-gate candidates are filtered, and tampered
stored bindings fail. Existing factual VSI/brief regressions, historical v6
loading and the five/six/twelve-plugin plus file/note save/reopen cases are retained.

These checks establish mechanical behavior, not semantic entailment or answer
quality. Exact quotes and lexical anchors cannot prove that a useful hypothesis
follows from its premises. The conservative vocabulary policy can also reject
reasonable paraphrases. Before activation, separately authorized real-model and
adversarial evaluation must assess supported novel hypotheses, irrelevant anchor
reuse, negation, attribution, scope/period ambiguity, invented mechanisms and
numeric/causal smuggling. Failure must keep the channel disabled rather than
relax source or numeric controls. No such evaluation or live acceptance occurred.

This is an inactive candidate, not full production readiness. The sandbox bridge's
current-data, isolated runtime, build, native locking and activation prerequisites
remain separate and unchanged.

Verified local result: 25 negative cases and the novel file/note positive pass;
actual mocked VSI/brief generation, protected save, stored loading and citation
reopening pass. VSI factual/history/privacy/accounting regressions, brief factual
and historical-v6 regressions, focused production admission guards and all three
five/six/twelve-provider file/note fixtures pass. TypeScript passes; targeted lint
has zero errors and one existing unused-import warning. Independent review's
same-row dependency and Unicode bypass findings are fixed and regression-tested.
Zero paid calls or external actions occurred. A full build of this new candidate
has not run; the existing Mac compiler limitation was not retried.
