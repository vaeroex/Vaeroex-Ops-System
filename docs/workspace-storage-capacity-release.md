# Workspace storage and paid capacity

This feature gives each workspace an independent 15,000,000,000-byte allowance and 500 original-file slots. No allowance is pooled across workspaces or businesses. Decimal GB are used for customer capacity; the per-original limit is 25 MiB (26,214,400 bytes).

Settings → Usage & Storage reports exact included/total allowances, retained original bytes, available capacity after reservations, active and archived files, ordinary Trash, protected historical Trash, and the 50 largest retained originals. Trash is separately paginated with one-based pages. Missing originals are shown as missing instead of consuming phantom file slots. Retained objects without records remain counted until reconciled. Database estimates are separately labeled and excluded from the paid file quota.

## Approved purchase terms

Terms version: `storage-capacity-v1`. The product owner approved release with an exact $5 USD first payment and $5 USD monthly renewal per pack, applicable tax included in that advertised price, and the exact first charge and renewal date shown before purchase. Activation still requires the revised payment flow's staging qualification and normal release checks.

- Each pack costs exactly $5 USD for the first payment and each monthly renewal and adds 10,000,000,000 bytes and 500 file slots to its selected workspace. Applicable tax is included; no extra tax or processing charge is added to the advertised price.
- Owners can choose additional capacity at every usage level. Self-service purchases are bounded to 10 committed packs. Each subscription's packs count once if it can still renew or has a paid invoice period that has not ended or been fully refunded. Disputed and future paid periods conservatively reserve their packs because they could later provide capacity. Fully refunded canceled subscriptions stop counting unless another qualifying paid period remains. Paid allowance can reach 115 GB and 5,500 file slots total. This is a file-capacity ceiling, not a promise of enterprise processing or integration throughput. Larger deployments require separate qualification.
- Each explicit purchase starts a separate monthly subscription. Before payment consent, the review shows new total allowance, recurring new/total charges, the exact first payment of `500 × packs` cents, the exact provider-confirmed renewal date/time, and cancellation effect. There is no first-period proration. Scheduled cancellations are excluded from the future recurring-charge total even when their paid capacity remains available. Unpaid/refunded subscriptions that can still renew remain in that recurring total. Preparing an unpaid review does not authorize a charge; payment confirmation stays unavailable until the owner accepts the recurring purchase. There is no automatic expansion, metered overage, trial, coupon, or billing credit path.
- The owner supplies a minimal billing address when requesting a review so Stripe can determine included tax. The address goes to Stripe for billing and tax calculation; Vaeroex does not save its raw fields in workspace records. Card details go directly to Stripe. Recurring-payment consent is never preselected and explicitly authorizes saving the payment method for these monthly renewals. The inclusive price and automatic-tax calculation must both be qualified in the release environment. A provider payment screen must not convert the advertised USD purchase into a different amount or currency; global Adaptive Pricing settings and eligible international payment locations are part of the final payment-surface qualification.
- Canceling renewal leaves capacity through its paid period. A failed renewal stops adding new paid periods. A partial refund leaves the period's capacity in place; a full refund withdraws that invoice's capacity. An active or lost payment dispute withdraws the disputed period. A verified won dispute or closed inquiry restores any still-current, unrefunded paid period. Provider timestamps and current dispute records prevent older event work from reversing newer reconciliation. Existing originals remain retained and restorable in every case; only new uploads that need unavailable capacity are blocked.
- The 10-pack purchase guard uses that per-subscription union plus the workspace's in-progress purchase. It does not add renewable and paid pack totals together or count the same subscription twice. This prevents a failed or disputed subscription's later recovery from silently exceeding the qualified operating limit. Refunding a payment does not itself cancel future renewal; the owner can cancel renewal separately. Commitment reserves the purchase ceiling only: disputed, unpaid, and future periods do not provide current paid capacity.
- A payment-success URL never grants capacity. Verified billing events reconcile provider objects into idempotent paid periods. Quantity, product, price, workspace, customer, subscription, invoice, currency, and successful charge attribution are checked before entitlement is granted.
- Persisted Stripe event receipts omit nested billing/shipping addresses, dispute-evidence addresses, payment client secrets, and hosted payment/document links on both first delivery and processing retries. Event IDs, amounts, periods, statuses, attribution metadata, and existing audit email fields remain available. Processing uses the original verified event in memory; receipt minimization does not change payment verification or capacity grants.
- Payment uses Stripe's official Payment Element against the finalized invoice's fixed-USD PaymentIntent. The scoped client secret is released only after explicit consent, kept transiently in the mounted form, and never logged or stored in URL/local storage. Card data goes directly through Stripe's hosted fields. Hosted invoice redirects are deliberately excluded: the qualified international probe selected EUR under global Adaptive Pricing despite a USD invoice. This feature does not change account-wide currency settings. See the official [PaymentIntent confirmation API](https://docs.stripe.com/js/payment_intents/confirm_payment) and [Stripe.js CSP guidance](https://docs.stripe.com/security/guide#content-security-policy).
- The final payment button states the exact USD amount and subscription. Duplicate submissions are blocked during card authentication. Declines allow an explicit retry; an interrupted or uncertain response prevents another submission from that form. `redirect: if_required` supports card authentication. The same-origin payment-return route strips provider parameters, applies `no-store` and `no-referrer`, and forwards only a validated workspace hint; switching workspaces never transfers payment or capacity. A return or client-reported success is never entitlement authority.
- Owners can resolve a failed renewal through an explicit review of the existing invoice and its current capacity period. Recovery reuses the fixed-USD payment form, does not buy new packs, and does not restart canceled renewals. The review discloses any still-scheduled renewal separately and releases payment credentials only after consent.

There is no manual-credit release step and no planned dormant purchase feature. The requested pricing/terms review is complete; live activation follows successful qualification of the revised exact-price payment flow and normal release checks.

## Lifecycle behavior

All retained originals consume bytes and file slots, including active files, archived files, Trash, and protected historical Trash. A new deletion moves the source to 30-day Trash; bytes and the file slot stay counted until confirmed physical deletion. This keeps restore available above the allowance while preventing Trash/restore from bypassing file capacity. Explicit permanent deletion requires confirmation and retries are visible as pending. Saved analyses/imported business records are not silently deleted with an original.

Pre-existing deleted objects are protected from permanent deletion, including manual purge. Their recovery/download controls remain available, pending an announced recovery/export window. No rollout operation should remove that protection or assign those records an automatic purge deadline without the approved notice process.

At 80% and 95% of byte or file capacity, the page warns proactively. At the allowance, existing access, downloads, search, exports, restoration of retained originals, deletion, operational work, integrations, and analyses remain governed by their existing permissions and separate processing limits. New uploads reserve bytes and a file slot atomically. Browser bytes go through a signed direct Storage PUT; only small metadata requests reach application routes/server actions. Unconfirmed transport outcomes stay blocked in the form and are not automatically retried.

## Rollback and incident operation

Clear `WORKSPACE_CAPACITY_PURCHASES_APPROVED_TERMS` and redeploy configuration to stop new live purchases. This does not cancel an already confirmed invoice payment attempt or existing subscription. Review affected unpaid initial invoices and incomplete subscriptions using current Stripe state; void only an explicitly selected unpaid initial invoice through Stripe and reconcile its normal event. Never void a paid invoice or an ordinary renewal as a broad rollback step. Legacy Checkout sessions, if any remain, retain their existing verified expiration path. Keep the paid-period ledger, webhook, owner cancellation, invoice recovery, retained file access, and maintenance running. Do not drop migrations, delete customer objects, change paid quantities by hand, or disable tenant/admission guards as a rollback shortcut.

For an application regression, deploy the last qualified revision that supports this schema, or make a reviewed forward fix. The previous direct-upload application is not compatible with the new reservation guard. Reconcile delayed provider events through the existing idempotent webhook after the fix; there is no manual capacity-credit procedure. Any customer refund or subscription cancellation must be an explicit provider action with its normal verified event and disclosed effect. Existing originals remain retained and restorable while new uploads over the resulting allowance are blocked.
