# Vaeroex Ops System

Vaeroex Ops System powers Vaeroex Executive Intelligence, an AI-assisted operations intelligence platform for small and mid-sized businesses.

Vaeroex helps business owners and managers improve visibility, accountability, and execution across key operational workflows, including forms, checklists, SOPs, follow-ups, issue tracking, asset readiness, CRM visibility, KPI reporting, and executive-level recommendations.

## Core Capabilities

- Business Health and operational visibility
- KPI tracking and performance reporting
- Forms, checklists, tasks, issues, assets, people, and SOP workflows
- Evidence-based findings and saved analyses
- Executive-level recommendations
- Subscription and workspace management
- Role-aware workspace access
- Secure authentication and workspace-scoped data access

## Integrations

Vaeroex supports and is continuing to expand integrations with business systems used by small and mid-sized companies.

Current and in-progress integration areas include:

- Square POS
- QuickBooks Online
- Additional POS, accounting, and business operations systems under development

## Product Focus

Vaeroex Executive Intelligence is designed to help businesses turn operational data into clearer decisions.

The platform focuses on structured inputs, evidence-based analysis, dashboard visibility, KPI reporting, workflow accountability, and practical recommendations that help leaders understand what is happening across the business.
